## Wood Veneer

Veneer refers to thin slices of wood and sometimes bark that typically are glued onto surface.

- Veneers are cut as thin as 0.64 mm (1⁄40 in).
- [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wood_veneer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wood_veneer)
- Historically, veneers were also sawn in approximately 3 mm (1⁄8 in) thick layers  
    - now they use either a specialized machine or lathe
