# Concrete

[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete)

Fluid cement that cures to a solid. It is the most manufactured material in the world. 

Cement-bound concrete is less durable/rigid that asphaltic concrete due to addition of petroleum consituent (bitumen, sticky black liquid)

Cement is seldom used on its own, but rather to bind sand and gravel together. They are inorganic (lacks carbon-hydrogen bonds), often lime- or calcium silicate-based.

Inorganic compounds comprise most of the Earth's crust.
