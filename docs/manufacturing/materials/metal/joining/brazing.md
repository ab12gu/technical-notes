# Brazing

[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazing)

It is a metal-joining process, where you take a lower-melting point metal in-between two metals to act as glue.

The metal flows thru via capillary action and is surrounded by flux to protect it from atmosphere. It is basically soldering but uses much stronger metal(s).
