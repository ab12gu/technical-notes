# Sheet Metal

[3 ways to cut sheet metal, ytb](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NEeS5nCh5e8)

- Plasma Cutter
- Angle Grinder
    - limited to straightlines
    - Con: Loud
    - 3 at tool library, 1 at bikery
- jigsaw
    - Ensure you have a metal cutting bit (finer teeth)
        - carbide blade
    - Con: Vibrates a lot
    - [jigsaw cap tool lib](https://capitolhill.myturn.com/library/inventory/show/308292)
    - [reciprocating saw cap tool lib](https://capitolhill.myturn.com/library/inventory/show/1029610)
- Oxy-asetaline Torch
    - Con: Too much heat into sheet metal
        - causes to warp
- Tin Snips
    - certain ones can be used for thin steel
    - tip: smaller cuts to bend metal less during cut
    - [ytb short](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/7_0IupWwTEY)
