# Fasting

Bryan Johnson Recommendation

- 10% calorie restriction
- 6-hour eating window
- 18-hour daily fast

## Body

0-3 hours

- using energy from food
- elevated insulin

3-8 hours

- fat oxidation increases
- liver converts gluecose to glycerin
- insulin falls

8-16 hours

- some fasting related cell signaling increases?

16-18+ hours

- ketone production increases?
- Fasting-related pathways such as AMPK and autophagy-related signaling may increase?
