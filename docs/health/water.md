## Test Supply

- Contact your water utility or a licensed plumber to find out if it’s made of lead. 
- Take extra precautions if it is, like using a filter certified to remove lead.
- Internal plumbing and faucets can also contain lead, so test at faucet

## Precaution

- You can flush for 30-45 seconds if pipes are old/rusty
- 1 min if you have old galvanized iron pipes
- lead service line, flush for 3-5 min

## Good Heavy Metals

At low concentrations, some heavy metals: 

- zinc
- iron
- copper
- manganese

are essential for human health. 

## Bad Heavy Metals

- lead
- arsenic
- cadmium
- mercury

have no biological role and can also be toxic.
