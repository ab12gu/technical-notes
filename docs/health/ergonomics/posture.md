# Posture

[https://blueprint.bryanjohnson.com/blogs/news/youre-likely-reading-this-with-poor-posture](https://blueprint.bryanjohnson.com/blogs/news/youre-likely-reading-this-with-poor-posture)

1. Imagine you have a string going up through your spine and head, and it's pulled straight up
2. Avoid bad posture when using phone
3. Move every 30 minutes throughout day
4. Strengthen posture muslces
    - shoulder shrugs! (1-2 times a week)
    - With a hand on your forehead, resist the pressure of your hand pushing your head back.
