# Omega 3

Omega-3 fatty acids is an umbrella term for polyunsaturated fatty acids PUFAs. 

DHA and EPA are two specific omega-3 fatty acids within this category relevant to humans.

## DHA

- DHA is major structural fat in the brain.
    - supports memory, focus and brain health
- It is the main fatty acid in the retina.
    - supports clear vision and long-term eye health

## EPA

- supports mood & healhty inflammation balance in the brain
- promotes healthy joints & mobility

## Sources

Recommended 400 mg of each EPA & DHA

- 2-3 ounces (55-85 grams) of fatty fish daily
    - salmon, sardines, mackerel, herring, and anchovies are most efficient

Plant sources like flax, chia or walnut don't provide meaningful ammounts due to needing conversion from alpha-linolenic acid (ALA)
