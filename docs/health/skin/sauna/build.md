# Build

I might build my own sauna!

Method for dry sauna uses conventional electric resistance heater with sauna stones (plunge that BJ uses)

Why Stones? Store heat rather than relying entirely on continuously heated air.

Plunge specs:

- Heater: HUUM DROP
- Heating power: 6 kW
- Stones: ~100–121 lbs
- Power supply: 240V
- Maximum temperature: 230°F (110°C)
