# Measurement

- Goal Internal Temp: 102.65*F
- General normal body temp: ~97.7*F
    - varies based on metabolism
    - lowest early in morning; lower in elderly
- Fever is commonly defined as 100.4*F (38*C) or higher
- Hypothermia: 95*F (35*C) or below

Ear/Rectal tend to measure higher; Oral/Armpit tend to measure under
