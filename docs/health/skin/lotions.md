2. NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide) 
    - NAD+ precursor
    - cellular energy & healthy aging pathways
    - supports skin barrior and antioxidant defenses
    - NMN skincare was released commercially around 2021 to 2022
        - primarily in Japanese and Korean skincare brands
        - but oral NMN was introduced in 2015
    - NMN is a processed subsection of Vitamin B3
    - Research effective at 2% to 5% across peer-reviewed trials
    - NOTE: Because NMN is hydrophilic (water-loving), the outer, oily layers of your skin naturally repel it
