# Skin

**TL;DR** Just go in the sun in morning/evening

## Basics

    + exercise
    + prioritize sleep
    + eat healthy
    + avoid junk, fried, or highly processed foods
    + cigarettes, and vaping
    + limit UV exposure
        + 90% of visible skin aging is from sun exposure, not sunburn

## Supplments

Consume 20-30 grams of collagen peptides daily (good to pair Viatmin C w/ peptides)

Others:

- Niacinamide
- Vitamin C
- Polyphenols and Antioxidants
- Omega-4 fatty acids

## Lotion

- [https://www.health.harvard.edu/staying-healthy/moisturizers-do-they-work](https://www.health.harvard.edu/staying-healthy/moisturizers-do-they-work)
- Mix of moisture soaking, moisture, and moisture blocking agents
    - Moisture soaks from the air and inner parts of skin
    - Most contain water as first ingredient
    - The grease blocks moisture from releasing from skin
