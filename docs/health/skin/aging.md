# Aging

Skin begins to age in your 20s!

## Collagen

Collagen content decreases about 1% annuallly, reducing skin firmness & suppleness. 

- Causes loss in moisture, reduced hyaluronic acid content, and declines in skin lipid content
    - results in dryness, reduced thickness, and diminished suppleness
- Fibroblasts, responsible for callogen and hyalouronic acid production, become less efficient (cellular aging)
    - impairs skin repair and water retention

## Elastin

Provides stretchability and prevents wrinkling, also deteriorates

## Environmental 

UV radiation and air pollutants breaks down collagen and induces oxidative stress
