# Therapies

1927 nm laser

- 1x every 6 months (or more)
- improves skin tone, texture, dyschromia, sun damage, fine lines
- reduces actinic keratoses and pigrmentation through accelerated epidermal turnover and renewal

1550 nm laser

- 1x every 6 months (or more)
- penetrades deeper into the mid-dermis
- creates controlled microthermal injury that stimulates fibroblasts to produce new collagen and elastin
- improvments in skin firmness, wrinkles, acne scars, overall structural integrity

Sofwave 

- 1x every 6 months (or more)
- non-invasive ultrasound skin tightening system
    - controlled energy into mid-dermis
- stimulates collagen and elastin production (without damaging surface)
- activates fibroblasts through precise thermal stimulation
- improves skin firmness, laxity, and fine lines (especially on face/neck)

Everesse

- 1x every 6 months (or more)
- monopolar radiofrequency device that delivers 6.78 MHz thermal energy 
- precisely to both papillary and reticular dermis simultaneously
- trigger a dual-phase college response:
    - immediate contraction of existing collagen fibers for visible lifting
    - sustained fibroplast activation that drives collagen remodelling and regeneration over months
- Results in lifting, volumizing and tightening the face/neck/jawline with no downtime
- Continuous water cooling throughout treatment potects epidermal surface

First two address surface damage/structural integrity

Second two stimulate collagen via termal energy but through different mechanism (RF adds lifting/volumizing)
