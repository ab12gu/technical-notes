# Sleep

- keep a consistent sleep schedule

## Caffeine

- caffeine at most 10 hours before sleep
    - 6 hour half life, so about 1/3 amount by 10 hours

## Wind Down routine

- dim lights/blue-light blocking glasses 1-2 hours before bed
- limit screens 1 hour before bed
- do a relaxing wind-down routine 60 min before bed (
    - read
    - walk
    - journal
    - hang out with family

## Sleep Temperture

- use a temperature-controlled mattress to stay cool
- bedroom temp: 60-67F
