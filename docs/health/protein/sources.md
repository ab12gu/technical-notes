# Sources

In general, minimize protein from foods like fatty beef, hot dogs, and deli meats.

Instead, try getting protein from:

    + eggs
    + nuts and seeds
    + unsweetened yogurt
    + lean chicken and turkey breast
    + legumes (lentils, chickpeas, beans)
    + fatty fish (salmon, mackerel, herring)

If you eat meat, consider swapping out some animal proteins for plant proteins to tip the plant-to-animal ratio in your favor.
