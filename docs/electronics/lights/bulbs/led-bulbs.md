## LED Bulbs

There are certain protocols that allow you to control the LED bulbs.

Most bulbs are developed offshore and they now produce corresponding software/hardware (for no added cost) cause it really doesn't cost them much...

Wattage

- Average LED Bulb: 8-10W
- 800 Lumens
- 1800K-6500K - range for white light

## Protocols

- Matter (new)
    - wifi
    - bluetooth
- Zigbee
    - sometimes requires hub to talk to them
