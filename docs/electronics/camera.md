# Camera

## Building a Camera

Atiyah wanted to do

- How can we make a compact small digital camera with the what you get is what you get feel? 
- Dreamy, nostalgic, saturated, unique, lofi distorted, what you get is what you get 

Camera specs (simple as possible)

- Copying the Harinezumi toy camera 

Specs

- Fixed 4mm f/3 lense 
- (38mm in 35mm format)

Options

- Color or monochrome 
- iOS: 100 or 800
- Normal or macro 

Materials

- Camera: Adafruit Chip
- Microcontroller: ESP32
