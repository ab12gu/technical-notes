# Tikka Masala

[https://youtu.be/_f4ArgoSmoM?si=fk3sJZ1_H4TcDlVd&t=218](https://youtu.be/_f4ArgoSmoM?si=fk3sJZ1_H4TcDlVd&t=218)

Marination:

- greek yogurt (1 cup)
- ginger (1/2 inch)
- garlic (4 cloves)
- garam masala (2 tsp)
- tumeric (1 tsp)
- paprika (1 tsp)
- ground cumin (1.5 tsp)
- pepper (1 tsp)
- salt (1 tsp)

Marinate for 20 min room or 24 hours in fridge

- Sear for 2 minutes each side
- Saute: onion, garlic (4 cloves), ginger (1in)
    - ground coriander (1 tsp), ground cumin (1 tsp), cayenne powder (1/2 tsp)
    - after onions soften:
        - broth/stock (1/2 cup), crushed tomatoes (1 15 ounce can)

Add chicken then add fats (half/half 1 cup, cream, etc)
