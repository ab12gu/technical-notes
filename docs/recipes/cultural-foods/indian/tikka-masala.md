# Tikka Masala

[https://youtu.be/_f4ArgoSmoM?si=fk3sJZ1_H4TcDlVd&t=218](https://youtu.be/_f4ArgoSmoM?si=fk3sJZ1_H4TcDlVd&t=218)

Marination:

- greek yogurt
- ginger
- garlic
- garam masala
- tumeric
- paprika
- ground cumin
- pepper
- salt

Marinate for 20 min room or 24 hours in fridge

- Sear for 2 minutes each side
- Saute: onion, garlic, ginger
    - ground coriander, ground cumin, cayenne powder
    - after onions soften:
        - broth/stock, crushed tomatoes

Add chicken then add fats (half/half, cream, etc)
