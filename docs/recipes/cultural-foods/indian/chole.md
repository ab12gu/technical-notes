# Chole

Necessary:

1. Cumin seeds
2. Coriander powder
3. Onion + tomato base
4. Ginger + garlic
5. Amchur/lemon for tang

## Ingredients

### Base

- cumin seeds
- ghee (or oil)
- onions, finely chopped
- tomatoes, pureed
- ginger
- garlic

### Core

- dried chickpeas

### Spices

- cumin seeds
- tumeric (1/2)
- red chili powder
- coriander powder
- green chilis

## Garnish

- cilantro
- lemon juice
- fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi)
- sliced red onion on side

### Add on

Optional fry an drizzle on top (before serving): 

- ghee
- cumin
- ping on of hing (asafoetida)
- chili powder

## Recipe

1. Soak chickpeas 6-8 hours overnight
    - cooks 2x faster, but can just cook in pressure cooker longer
    - may cook more even... unsure
2. Drain water, instapot for 30+ minutes?
    - optional soak 1-2 teabags in it
    - use cooked liquid as broth

NOTE: To flavor beans when boiling:

- 1-2 bay leaves
- cinnamon stick
- black cardamon
- salt

Beans contain sugars called `raffinose-family oligosaccharides` (raffinose, stachyose, verbascose)

- humans cant digest these sugars, so produces gas
- soaking dissolve sugar into water, losing them when draining water
- in your gut it is fermented and turns into short-chain fatty acids for you
- tolerance build up overtime

### Masala

3. Ghee/oil + cumin seeds (bay leaf & cinnamon)
4. Onions till golden
5. Ginger-garlic for 1 minute
6. Tomatoes until thick/oil separating
7. Add spices
    - coriander, tumeric, chili powder, salt
    - 2-3 minutes (may need water to prevent burning)

### Mix

8. Add chickpeas
    - Cook for 15-20 minutes
9. Add garam masala + amchur/lemon + chopped cilantro at end
    - loses flavor if heated too long
