# Larb

[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larb)

It is a type of Lao meat salad that is the national dish (also popular in Thailand). Larb (or Laab) is minced or pounded meat. 

- flavored with fish sauce, lime juice, padaek, roasted ground rice and fresh herbs
- it is minced and mixed with chili, mint
