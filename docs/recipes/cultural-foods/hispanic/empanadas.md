# Empanadas

- [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RRNVLC6IAv4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RRNVLC6IAv4)
- [https://preppykitchen.com/empanadas/](https://preppykitchen.com/empanadas/)

## Ingredients

- Celery
- Carrots
- Potatoes
- Ground Beef (something fatty)
- Onion
- Garlic (add later)
- Tomato paste (add later)
- frozen peas (add later)


## Spices

- paprika or chili powder
- peppers
- cumin
- salt
- pepper
- cinnamon!!!

## Dough

- Flour (all-purpose)
- Butter
- Egg inside (and on rubbed on top before baking)
- water

## Cooking

1. Fry ingredients
    - Add garlic later
2. Make dough via food processor or hand
3. Oven at 375 for 30+ minutes
