# Berries

Contains flavonoids:

    + anthocyanins
    + flavonols
    + isoflavonoids)

along with: 

    + fiber
    + tannins
    + vitamin C
    + folate,
    + potassium


Eating cranberries is linked with a 31% lower risk of death from any cause and a 51% lower risk of cardiometabolic death.

Blueberries and strawberries, which have a significant amount of flavonoids, have been shown to support brain health in older adults.*

Berries can support healthy:

    + cholesterol
    + blood pressure
    + blood sugar

A daily cup of blueberries could promote healthy arteries and blood flow.

All of this contributes to supporting your heart health.
