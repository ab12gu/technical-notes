# Chips

## Sweet Potato Chips

[https://www.aspicyperspective.com/baked-sweet-potato-chips/](https://www.aspicyperspective.com/baked-sweet-potato-chips/)

- Chip via knife or mandolin slicer
    - Mandolin slicer allows for uniform cuts to cook chips evenly
- Cook at 300F for 30 min

Spices: (toss)

- olive oil
- paprika
- garlic powder
