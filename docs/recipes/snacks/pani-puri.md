# Pani Puri

Popular Indian street food

Crispy, hallow, fried dough balls (puri) stuffed with:

- boiled potatoes
- steamed moong sprouts
- boiled chickpeas
- white peas curry (ragda)

Served with spicy tangy water & sweet chutney (too much sugar)

## Puri

- You can buy prefried puri's at indian store
- or buy dough flats to drop into oil
- or make your own dough!

## Pani

Blend ingredients till smooth (water as needed)

- 2:1 cilantro to mint
    - only mint leaves, stems are bitter
    - cilantro stems are fine
- 1 tbs Tamarind (fruit)
    - or lemon juice to get tangy flavor
- 1 tbp Roasted cumin powder
    - roast and then mash in morter/pestle
- 2 chopped dates, 2 tbp jaggery
    - or double dates (not a fan of jaggery sugar)
- 1 tsp chaat masala
- salt for taste (black or edible rock salt?)

Optional: Add boundi 

## Filling

Mixed spices and potato

- 2-3 sweet potatoes (ur choice)
    - 1 tbp chopped coriendar leaves
    - 1/4 tsp red chili powder
    - 1 tsp chaat masala
    - 1 tsp roasted cumin powder
    - salt to taste

Steamed moong sprouts
