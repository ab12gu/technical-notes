# Nutty Pudding

[https://blueprint.bryanjohnson.com/blogs/news/bryan-johnsons-nutty-pudding-recipe](https://blueprint.bryanjohnson.com/blogs/news/bryan-johnsons-nutty-pudding-recipe)

## Ingredients

- ½ tsp of ceylon cinnamon
    - General cinnamon is cassia, stronger, spicer (eastern/southeast Asia origin)
        - at high levels has liver issues
    - vs ceylon is considered "true" mildly sweet/floral notes (south India origin, harder to harvest)
- 1 tsp ground flaxseeds
- 1 tsp sunflower lecithin
- 2 tsp of ground walnuts
- ¼ cup of berries (strawberries, raspberries, blackberries, or blueberries)
- 3 cherries
- 3 tbsp ground macadamia nuts
- ¼ of a brazil nut
- 50-100 ml of nut milk, depending on what consistency you like (I prefer 50 ml)
- 60 ml of pomegranate juice

## Extra

- 2 tbsp chia seeds
- 30-60 g protein
- 1/4 cup of berries
