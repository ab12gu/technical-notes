# Pizza

[Tawa Pizza, no oven or yeast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zAICu_dIlUs)

## Steps

1. Make dough
    - Knead for 5 min to turn soft not sticky
    - Cover & set aside for 1 hour
2. Grill veggies in pan then set aside
3. Make sauce
    - saute onion/garlic before adding tomato
4. Flatten dough & prick with fork
    - add sauce + veggies + cheese, grill

## Ingredients

Dough

- 1 cup All purpose flour
- 1/3 cup Wheat flour
- 1/2 tsp Baking powder
- 1/2 tsp Baking soda
- 1 tbsp Oil 
- Salt to taste
- 3 tbsp Curd/Yogurt
- water

For Topping

- 2 tbsp Olive oil
- 2 Capsicium Sliced (bell pepper)
- 1 Onion, Sliced
- 3-4 Mushroom, Sliced
- Salt to taste
- 1 tsp Chilli flakes
- 1 tsp Oregano
- 4-5 Basil leaves
- 1 Tomato, sliced (add last)

For Sauce

- 1 tbsp Oil
- 2-3 Garlic, finely hopped
- 1 medium Onion, finely chopped
- 1/2 cup fresh Tomato Puree
    - finely chopped probably works
- 1/2 tsp Sugar
- 1/2 tsp Vinegar
- Salt to taste
- 1/2 tsp Oregano
- 1/2 tsp Chilli flakes
- 1/3 cup Mozzarella cheese or Pizza cheese, grated
