# Frittata

Egg-based Italian Dish

1. Saute Veggies
2. Add raw eggs to baking tray
3. Bake for like an hour

The more you whisk the eggs, the more air gets incorporated, so more fluffy :)

## Ingredients

Veggies:

- mushrooms
- onions
- bell peppers

Extra veggies (add later in saute):

- garlic
- spinach (cook until spinach wilts)

Eggs: (wisk with)

- 6 eggs
- 1/2 cup greek yogurt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1/4 tsp tumeric
- salt

Extra:

- broccoli
- peas
- edamame
- sweet potato/barley
- feta
- cumin, coriander, chiliflakes, cilantro
