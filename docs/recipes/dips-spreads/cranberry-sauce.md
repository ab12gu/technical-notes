# Cranberry Sauce

[Kenji YTB - Sauce Recipe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GNaTZCoqplE)

Preserves for weeks because cranberries are very acidic. Flavors better as it rests!

Time: 10-20 min

## Ingredients

- sugar (stevia seemed good)
- cranberries
- water
- orange (single)
- star anise (single)
- cinnamon stick (single)

ADD Ons:

- wine (red/port)
- apple cider
    - apple cider vinegar
- lemon juice/zest
- pomegranite juice/seeds
    - or any berry/fruit
- pineapple juice

Spices:

- fresh ginger
- nutmeg
- ground cloves
- allspice
- cardamon
- vanilla extract 
- black pepper
- cayenne pepper

Alts:

- nuts!** (sounds great!)
    - walnuts
    - peacans
    - almonds
    - pistachios
    - chia seeds
- aromatics:
    - shallots
    - red onion
    - fresh rosemary
    - thyme
    - sage
    - mint** (sounds great)


## Steps

- just simmer till cranberries mesh into liquid
- might need to smush orange if cut up
