# Hing

[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asafoetida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asafoetida)

Dried latex (gum oleoresin), often not liked for its odour, but used in Worcesterschire sauce. 

Often complements or substitute for alliaceous ingredients such as onion and garlic

Only 1/15th is sufficient to season a dish. You can do a '`tarka` by frying it in some oil before adding on dish. Aroma of this spice will last several years. 

## Indian dishes

Often used in south india in rasam, sambar, daal, or urad bean flour. Woah, also component of chaat masala and snacks from bombay. It allows for Jains/Brahmins to get onion/garlic flavor without breakign their religion. 

Add to Daal, Rajma, Chole, Jeera Rice, Papad (lentil wafers)
