1. Sauté half an onion and jalapeño in a large pot on medium heat
2. After Onion softens add:
	- cumin or cumin seeds
	- drained beans
    - garlic gloves smashed
    - bay leaf
	- water
	- salt and pepper
3. Optional
	- oregano
    - smoked paprika
    - jalapeno
    - chili powder
4. Cook until beans are falling apart and the liquid  has thickened (at least 1 hr)
5. Season:
	- squeeze of lime juice
    - some vinegar
	- more salt and pepper
	- chili powder
6. Garnish the pinto beans with cilantro and red pepper flakes
