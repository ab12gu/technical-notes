# Beans

[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bean)

- Generally a good idea to wash beans to remove dust. 
- Ratio: 3 cups of water to 1 cup beans

## Soak

Place beans in cold water (no salt needed here)

1. Soak on counter for 8-12 hours
2. After 12 hours, either refridgerate or get new water
    - else beans will ferment (growth of bacteria)
    - these cause upset stomach or food poisoning

## Quick Soak
    
Boil beans in water for 2 minutes, then remove from heat and soak for 1 hour.

1. Boil for 90 minutes (15 minutes less for black beans)
2. Drain beans
3. Add  acid (vinegar, tomatoes, lemon/lime) to avoid beans toughening
4. 5 day fridge life

Add Garlic, onion, bay leaves, herbs for taste
