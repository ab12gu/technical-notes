## Henry's Recipe:

**Southern-Style Collard Greens**

*Crowd-sized batch · Smoky, tender, slow-braised greens*

### Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Fresh collard greens | 4–5 lbs |
| Smoked ham hocks | 2 large |
| Low-sodium chicken stock | 6–8 cups |
| Yellow onion | 1 large, diced |
| Garlic | 4–6 cloves, minced |
| Apple cider vinegar | ¼ cup |
| Brown or white sugar | 1–2 tbsp |
| Crushed red pepper flakes | 1–2 tsp |
| Butter | 2–4 tbsp |
| Kosher salt | To taste |
| Black pepper | To taste |
| Cooking oil | Splash |

### 1. Prep and Wash the Greens

*Approximately 20 minutes*

1. Fold each leaf in half lengthwise and remove the tough center rib.
2. Stack 6–8 leaves, roll them like a cigar, and slice crosswise into approximately 1.5-inch ribbons.
3. Depending on leaf size, slice once or twice in the other direction.
4. Fill a clean sink or large bowl with cold water and swish the greens around to dislodge sand and grit.
5. Let them sit briefly, then lift the greens out of the water rather than pouring through a colander.
6. Repeat washing if the water looks dirty.

### 2. Build the Smoky Broth

*45–60 minutes*

1. Sauté the diced onion in a splash of oil over medium heat until soft, about 5–7 minutes.
2. Add the minced garlic and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add the ham hocks and chicken stock, using enough stock to cover the hocks by a couple of inches.
4. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a gentle simmer.
5. Partially cover and simmer for 45–60 minutes to develop the smoky broth and soften the ham hocks.

### 3. Braise the Greens

*1–1.5 hours*

1. Add the collards in 2–3 batches, pushing each batch down with tongs until it wilts enough to fit the next.
2. Stir in the apple cider vinegar, crushed red pepper flakes, and sugar.
3. Simmer gently, partially covered, for 1–1.5 hours.
4. Stir occasionally and taste for tenderness. The greens should be silky and tender with no noticeable chewiness.

### 4. Shred the Ham and Finish

1. Remove the ham hocks from the pot and let them cool for approximately 5 minutes.
2. Pull off the tender meat and chop it into bite-sized pieces.
3. Discard the bones, skin, and large pieces of fat.
4. Stir the meat back into the greens.
5. Stir in 2–4 tablespoons of butter, to taste.
6. Season with kosher salt and freshly cracked black pepper.
7. Add an extra splash of vinegar or hot sauce if needed to brighten the flavor.

**Season with salt at the very end**, after the ham hocks have simmered and the butter has been incorporated, since the ham and stock can contribute substantial salt.

### 5. Cool and Store

1. Divide the greens into shallow containers with plenty of pot liquor.
2. Refrigerate promptly rather than leaving the large pot out to cool for an extended period.

For food safety, refrigerate within 2 hours of cooking (1 hour if the ambient temperature is above 90°F / 32°C).

## Serving

Serve warm with plenty of pot liquor. Optional accompaniments include cornbread, hot sauce, extra apple cider vinegar, black-eyed peas, fried chicken, or barbecue.
