# Stir Fry 

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jRt_j6d7pAQ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jRt_j6d7pAQ)

![](https://www.myplantifulcooking.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/eggplant-stir-fry-plate.jpg){:style="width: 300px"}

1. Chop eggplant
2. Soak Eggplant in Salt
3. Drain, sprinkle cornstarch
4. Fry solo
5. Fry ginger, garlic, chili peppers
6. Add sauce and eggplant

## Ingredients

- 2 Chinese eggplants
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1 ½ tbsp cornstarch
- 5 chopped garlic cloves 
- 1 tsp finely chopped ginger
- 1 red chili pepper
- 1 green onion
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil

## Sauce

- 2 tbsp light sodium soy sauce (do mot use the regular sodium soy sauce. It will then be too salty)
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce (for vegan recipes, use mushroom sauce instead of oyster sauce)
- 2 tbsp black vinegar
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tsp dobanjang
- 2 tbsp shaoxing wine
- 4 tbsp water
