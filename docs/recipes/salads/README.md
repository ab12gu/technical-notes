# Salads

[https://blueprint.bryanjohnson.com/blogs/news/macadamia-nut-slaw-recipe](https://blueprint.bryanjohnson.com/blogs/news/macadamia-nut-slaw-recipe)

Ingredients (4 servings):

    + 4 cups purple cabbage
    + 2 medium carrots
    + 1 apple
    + 2 scallions
    + 1/3 cup Raw Macadamias
    + 2 tbsp pumpkin seeds
    + 2 tbsp tahini
    + juice of 1 lime
    + 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    + small handful cilantro
    + pinch of salt
    + 1 tbsp Extra Virgin Olive Oil (optional)
