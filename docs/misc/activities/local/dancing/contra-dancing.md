# Contra Dancing

[https://www.seattledance.org/contra/emeraldcity/](https://www.seattledance.org/contra/emeraldcity/)

- Phinney Ridge Neighborhood
- [6532 Phinney Avenue North, Seattle 98103](https://www.google.com/maps/@47.6773937,-122.352939,19z?entry=ttu)
    - Community Hall of the brick building, near the lower parking lot
- Friday nights
- Beginner’s workshop at 7:00 pm. Dance from 7:30 pm to 10:00 pm.
- They go to a townhouse nearby after and eat/drink

Cost

– Sliding scale $10-$20.
– Students $5 with a school ID.
– Free for people under the age of 16.

Notes

- Admission limited to 140 people
- If this is your first contra dance ever you’ll get a coupon for free admission to your next dance
