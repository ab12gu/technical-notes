# Animals

## Fish

Salmon Hatchery Issaquiah 

- [https://www.issaquahfish.org/plan-your-visit/](https://www.issaquahfish.org/plan-your-visit/)
- Open year-round
- Open daily from 8:30am – 4:00pm 
- Self-guided tours start at the aquarium room and last about 45-90 minutes
- Tours are $10 a person & 50 minutes and happen rain or shine on Mon & Wed
- The best time to view adult salmon at the hatchery:
    - September and mid-November
    - First Chinook arrive in late August
    - First coho return in late September
    - In early October you are likely to see two species of salmon: Chinook (king) and coho (silver). 
    - Occasionally, sockeye, pink and kokanee have appeared in Issaquah Creek.
