# Salsa

[wiki/salsa_(dance)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salsa_(dance))

Latin american dances to salsa music. It was popularized in NY in 1960s/70s by a Dominican artist as a form of Afro-Caribbean dance rooting in NY and Cuba. It is developed from Afro-Cuban and Caribbean social dances (Son cubano, danzon, mambo, cha-cha-cha and rumba) and American dances such as swing and tap.

Salsa tempo ranges from 150 bpm to around 250 bpm (dancing typically between 160-220 bpm)

Two styles:

1. Linear
- similar to West Coast Swing
- NY style & LA style
- New York style is danced on the second beat of the music ("on 2")
    - the follower, not the leader, steps forward on the first measure of the music
- greater emphasis on performing "shines"
    - origins from Swing and New York Tap
2. Circular
- simlar to East Caost Swing
- Cuban & Colombian style
