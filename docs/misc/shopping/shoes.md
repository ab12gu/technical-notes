## Rant

I wear `barefoot` shoes, where there is a wide toe box, no heel to toe drop (flat), and no arch support. 

The heel to toe drop you have, the more you use your quads to move your legs and reducing this puts more pressure on your calves. In my opinion, this just allows you to walk barefeet comfortably, because it mimics the natural formfactor of human walking/running :). 

I am already pretty tall @ 6'2", but Chistian is kinda annoying me saying that I'm not tall, so I might just get the `barefoot` shoes with a giant cushion underneath so I look taller to fuck with him. I also think that you wear down the natural cushion below your feet when you run without cushions on asphalt/cement that I do when I run in the city with Kiran.
