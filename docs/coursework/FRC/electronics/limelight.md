# Limelight

[Limelight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limelight) is the word used for stagelighting used to focus on specific object/actor in theatre. This has nothing to do with the camera, just a quirky name!

They are a camera + microcontroller used to generally perform auto-aiming and auto-movement. 

Standard vision sensor (camera) used in FRC. <br>

## Limelight 4

[https://limelightvision.io/products/limelight-4](https://limelightvision.io/products/limelight-4)

- Price: $450
- Sensor: OV9281 Global Shutter Monochrome 
    - 1280x800 @ 120FPS
    - 640x400 @ 240FPS
- Power
    - Input: 3.3V - 24V / 6S (35V absolute maximum)
    - 12W maximum power consumption
    - Use a 5A or 10A breaker
    - 18-20AWG wires
- Replaceable M12 Lens
    - 82 deg Horizontal FOV
    - 56.2 deg Vertical FOV
- Powered by: Rasberry Pi CM5
- Built in IMU

[https://docs.limelightvision.io/docs/docs-limelight/getting-started/limelight-4](https://docs.limelightvision.io/docs/docs-limelight/getting-started/limelight-4)

## Limelight 3

[https://limelightvision.io/products/limelight-3](https://limelightvision.io/products/limelight-3) 

- Cost: $400
- Sensor: OV5647 Color Rolling Shutter
    - 90FPS Capture @ 640x480, 
    - 60FPS Capture @ 1280x960
- Power:
    - 4.1V-24V, 30V absolute maximum
    - 7W maximum power consumption
    - 5A breaker
- Status Lights: 
    - Green (target detection)
    - Yellow (network status)
- LED Illumination: 600 lumens, green illumination
