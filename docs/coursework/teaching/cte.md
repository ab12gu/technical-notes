# CTE

Career and Technical Education (CTE) teacher certificate

[https://ospi.k12.wa.us/certification/first-time-certification/cte-teacher](https://ospi.k12.wa.us/certification/first-time-certification/cte-teacher)

## Requirements

- Two years of certified teaching/coordination in the CTE-related subject area 
    - with an authorized employer, e.g., school district or skills center
- A total of 6,000 hours of experience is required; 
    - 2,000 hours MUST be occupational industry experience (non-teaching). 
        - Roughly a year full-time
    - Candidates may use up to 4,000 hours of teaching experience 
        - in the related Career and Technical Education Specialty program area(s).
    - But all 6,000 can come from work experience (including internships)
